## Guide slide images and step-by-step marks (2026-11-04)
- **Rendering:** `soffice` IS installed now (/opt/homebrew/bin/soffice, LibreOffice 26.8; the earlier "not installed" notes are out of date), so all 26 revised guides (485 slides) were rendered pptx -> PDF -> PNG with LibreOffice + pypdfium2 (pure Python, permissive licence; PyMuPDF was avoided for its AGPL licence). The embedded-pictures fallback (`GI.build_pictures`) exists for machines without LibreOffice and is not used here. Images: 1280 px wide PNG + 360 px thumbnail, `data/processed/guide_images/<guide_id>/` (git-ignored, 192 MB).
- **The rendered slide is the whole slide** (its step text, footer and the picture with the labels and leader lines drawn on it), because the instruction was to render the slides and keep labels and arrows. So the student sees the step text twice (beside the picture and in the steps list); a crop to the picture area was NOT done (it would need a per-slide decision).
- **Which slides get an image:** slides with pictures or diagrams, plus image-only stations. Text-only slides are rendered but not linked (an image of plain text adds nothing). Station -> images by the slide numbers the station already stores.
- **Serving:** `GET /api/guides/{id}/slides/{n}/image|thumb`, any signed-in user with access to the guide (403 otherwise, an instructor may open all, 404 for a slide without an image), `Cache-Control: private, max-age=3600`.
- **Marks:** normalised 0-1 coordinates, per slide, optional step (0-based within the station; null = shown with every step), draft/approved, author, version, history table; an edit of an approved mark sends it back to draft; approve is per station (every mark on the station's slides). Students get approved marks only, with no author/version/status.
- **Auto-draft** reads python-pptx shapes: connectors/lines with an arrowhead -> arrow; dashed lines -> cut line; block arrows -> arrow; unfilled ellipses -> highlight; short non-placeholder text boxes (not footers) -> label pins. A plain leader line from a label to a structure moves the pin to the STRUCTURE end (the slide image already shows the label text). Group transforms are applied; rotation is not. Steps are matched by label words (>= 60% of the label's words appear in a step); arrows/circles take the step of the nearest matched label (within 0.25). Result on the 26 real guides: 57 draft marks (37 labels, 19 arrows, 1 cut line), 35 linked to a step, 0 approved. Few guides have shapes at all: most labels are part of the flattened picture.
- **Chat image cards:** the card is built from the guide slides the answer cited (class number and slide numbers parsed from the citation), only for guides the student may open; procedural answers (2+ numbered steps) get one card per cited step (max 8), concept answers one card. A card's marks are the approved marks of that slide, narrowed to the answer step's station step when the answer text matches a step (>= 35% word overlap), else all approved marks of the slide. `images` is on Message (not on each Source) because a card is per step, not per source.
- **Lightbox** is a Radix dialog: wheel and pinch zoom, buttons, drag to pan when zoomed, double click toggles, arrows between images, Esc closes; the marks are an SVG inside the zoomed layer.
- **Student layout:** steps left, image right on large screens; on phones the image comes first, then the steps. In a station with approved marks the steps are buttons (one selected step at a time) and Next/Back move through steps and then stations; without approved marks the station behaves as before.
- **Instructor editor** lives in Notes > a published guide > "Image marks": draw arrow / cut line / circle / label pin, assign to a step, edit text and step, delete, reorder, approve or send back a whole station, preview the student view. Geometry is edited by deleting and redrawing (no handles).
- **Replacing a guide file** in the Notes manager re-renders its images (when the original pptx and LibreOffice are there) and sends every approved mark back to draft.
- **.doc upload** is now enabled (converted to .docx with LibreOffice); it was refused before only because soffice was believed missing.

## Guide pictures polish: crops, OCR drafts, editor (2026-11-04, later)
- **Crop from the pptx shapes, not by guessing from pixels.** Picture / diagram shapes (groups and rotation applied) are clustered (touching ones merge: one figure; separate ones stay separate crops in reading order); label, arrow, line, ellipse and freeform shapes that touch a cluster are added; text boxes with step text (numbered or more than 6 words), headers, footers, slide numbers and title / closing slides (university logo) are never added. A short phrase that a step also mentions is a LABEL, not step text. The PDF page is re-rendered per crop so the crop is at least 1600 px on its long side (page width capped at 7000 px). Format WebP (q92) instead of PNG: about a third the size, labels stay crisp.
- **Confidence:** "high" = from picture shapes with no step text over the picture; "low" = flattened slide (a picture over 70% of the slide: OCR paints out the step text lines and the non-background region is the illustration), step text overlapping the picture, a crop covering almost the whole slide, or a tiny picture. A low-confidence crop is HIDDEN from students (404) until the instructor presses "Mark as checked" in the editor. On the 26 real guides no slide was flattened and none was low: the flattened path is covered by a unit test only.
- **Original slide:** the whole slide (1280 px PNG) is kept for instructors only (`/slides/{n}/original`, 403 for students even with a grant). The old whole-slide student routes were removed. All picture URLs need a signed-in user with a grant, are `private` and `noindex` (some slides carry third-party atlas art: Netter plates, Lippincott credit lines are part of the pictures and are kept).
- **Marks now live in CROP coordinates** with a `crop` number; older marks (slide coordinates) are moved onto the crop that holds them; partly outside = converted, draft, flagged; outside every crop = flagged draft that bulk approve never approves; geometry edits clear the flag. Real data: 55 auto drafts moved, then the auto drafts were redone from the pptx.
- **OCR drafts:** Apple Vision through ocrmac (local, no download) is the OCR backend on this Mac (`src/ocr.py`; pytesseract only if tesseract exists; otherwise the step is skipped with a message). A line becomes a candidate label when it has 6 words or fewer, is not step text or a number, and at least 60% of its 4+ letter words are in the guide's or its lecture notes' vocabulary; stacked lines are merged ("Great / Saphenous / Vein"). A leader line found by Hough lines gives the pin on the structure plus an arrow; with no leader, a pin is made only when the label matches a step (otherwise it would just repeat the text in the picture). All are DRAFTS (source `ocr`); OCR drafts are redone separately from slide-shape drafts and never touch approved or hand-made marks. Bug caught in the live run: the OCR script treated its own earlier drafts as "already there" and deleted them; fixed.
- **Rotation:** lines, block arrows, ellipses and picture boxes are rotated about their centre (a quarter turn swaps an ellipse's radii; other angles use the unrotated box and are for the instructor to check).
- **Explain simply:** at most 4 sentences / 90 words from the station text and the lecture-notes passages the student may open (usable text only: lines under review are handled), body type only where the sources discuss it, a number that is not in the sources rejects the answer (one retry), cached per station, body type and grants, labelled "AI explanation, check your guide". Student tool.
- **Editor:** drag to move, square handles to resize or reshape, arrow keys nudge (Shift = bigger), Delete removes, 50-step undo / redo (a deleted mark comes back as a draft), approve-station opens a preview with every draft and its picture and lists flagged marks and unchecked pictures first.
- **Live check as student1** (`scripts/guide_images_live_check.py`, real index, throw-away copies of users, sessions, chats and marks; real files only read): granted station crops 200 (1051 x 1600 px), ungranted guide 403 (its picture 403), no sign-in 401, whole slide 403 for a student, every mark sent is approved. **The BullsAI gateway was unreachable during the final run** (network / VPN), so the model-written procedural answer and Explain simply were NOT run live: the cards were built from the real retrieval for the question plus the real card builder instead (6 cards, all crops 200, only approved marks).
